About Us

More than two decades of trusted expertise in chartered accounting, built on integrity and excellence across Malaysia and beyond.

Who We Are

A Legacy of Professional
Excellence Since 2003

RW William (RW) was formed in the year 2003, in Petaling Jaya, Selangor Darul Ehsan, as a chartered accounting firm after having obtained the relevant statutory approvals from the Malaysian Treasury, the Malaysian Institute of Accountants and the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia.

The formation of RW stems from the vision of its founder member, Mr. Richard William, whose initial and on-going objectives have been to provide a higher standard of services and efficiency to various clients and to provide a large firm know-how to smaller businesses.

"RW WILLIAM PLT (201906003458 (LLP0022270-LCA) & AF 1490) was registered on 19 November 2019 and with effect from that date, RW WILLIAM (AF 1490), a conventional partnership was converted to a limited liability partnership."

Today, RW has established its own network of professional firms as RW William Network. With offices in Petaling Jaya, Penang, Seremban, Johor Bahru, Ipoh and Klang comprising 3 partners and above 50 professional and dedicated staff, RW serves more than 2,000 clients throughout Malaysia.

Drawing on our combined comprehensive range of skills and expertise coupled with our sense of professional duty, our principal objectives will continue to be maximising client satisfaction by providing personal service and focusing on the particular goals and objectives of our clients, to help their business grow.

To this end, we seek to alleviate, if not eliminate, problems faced by our clients by presenting imaginative, sound and feasible proposals that will best address our clients' needs.
